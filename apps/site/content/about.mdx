# About CauseDeck

CauseDeck is software for advocates. It helps you keep track of your cases, read cause lists and
prepare for the court day.

## What the software does

- Keeps case status, orders and notices in one place.
- Shows the cause lists for the courts you follow.
- Stores customer data in India.

CauseDeck is software, not a legal service. It does not give legal advice.

That is the whole position: software for advocates, not legal services.
