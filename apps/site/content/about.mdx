# About CauseDeck

CauseDeck is software for advocates. It reads the cause lists and orders you already receive, prepares
a brief for each listing and keeps track of every direction you owe the court or your client. It is
built in India, for practice in the Madras High Court and the Karnataka High Court to start with, and
your data stays in India.

CauseDeck is a tool in the advocate's hands. The advocate confirms, the advocate sends and the
advocate files. The software prepares and proposes.

## What CauseDeck is

- Software you subscribe to, priced per seat in rupees. See [pricing](/pricing/).
- A court-day board, briefs and a list of obligations, each fact shown with the line of the order it came from.
- A workspace for one advocate or a whole chamber, with a restricted seat for a clerk.

## What CauseDeck is not

This list is permanent. It is not a phase of the roadmap.

- **Not a legal service.** CauseDeck does not give legal advice and has no lawyer-client relationship with anyone. It does not appear in court.
- **No directory of lawyers.** Nobody can look up an advocate on CauseDeck.
- **No ratings or rankings.** The software does not rate, rank or compare lawyers.
- **No lead generation.** CauseDeck does not advertise advocates, collect enquiries for them or send litigants to them, and it takes no referral fees.
- **No generated citations.** A citation in the product comes from you or from a source the software links to. It is never invented.
- **No judge analytics.** CauseDeck does not score or profile judges.
- **No autonomous filing.** Nothing is sent or filed without you.

## Who it is for

Practising advocates, juniors, chamber owners and their clerks. CauseDeck does not speak to litigants.
