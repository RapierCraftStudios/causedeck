### Added

- A court-day board with halls as columns, so tomorrow's listings are visible in one view.
- Briefs prepared for each listing, with the line of the order that each fact came from.
- A capture inbox for orders and lists you already receive: uploads, forwarded email, WhatsApp exports and calendar files.
- An activity feed for changes the software makes on your behalf, with undo for seven days.
- Workspace export, and workspace deletion with a 14-day cool-off.
- India-only AI mode, on by default for every workspace.
- Plans for Junior, Advocate and Chamber seats, with a clerk seat on Chamber, and UPI Autopay.
