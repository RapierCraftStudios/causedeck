- Plan the day the evening before: open the cause list, note each item number and court hall.
- Carry what each item needs: the file, the last order and any memo of appearance.
- After the hearing, record the next date and the direction in your diary the same day.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
