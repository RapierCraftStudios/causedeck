- The registry scrutinises each filing and returns it with a list of defects such as missing signatures, court fee or annexures.
- Cure every listed defect and re-present within the time the registry allows; keep a copy of the defect note.
- If you disagree with a defect, ask for the matter to be listed before the court rather than ignoring it.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
