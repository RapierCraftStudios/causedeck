- Limitation is the period within which a proceeding must be started or an appeal filed; the clock usually runs from a defined event, such as the date of the order.
- Work out the last date from the governing statute, and diary it with a margin before it.
- A delay can sometimes be condoned on an application showing sufficient cause, but never plan on it.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
