- A matter is listed on a date the registry or the court fixes; a mention asks the court to take up an item sooner or out of turn.
- Mention only with a stated reason, in the form the court prescribes, and tell the opposite side.
- Note the court's reply in the diary: a new date, a refusal, or a direction to file an application.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
