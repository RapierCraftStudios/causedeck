- A cause list orders matters by court hall, then by item number; the bench and the sitting time sit at the top.
- Read the case number, the party names and the advocate column together before you assume an item is yours.
- Supplementary and advance lists can add or move items: check them again in the morning.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
