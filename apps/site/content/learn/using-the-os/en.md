- Start with the court-day view: it shows tomorrow's items from the cause lists for the courts you follow.
- Confirm discovered matters once, then let alerts tell you when a date, a listing or an order changes.
- Every date you act on should come from a source you can open; check the source when the stakes are high.

> This lesson is general education, not legal advice. Court rules and practice directions change; check the current rules of the court and consult a practising advocate.
