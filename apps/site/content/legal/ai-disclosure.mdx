# AI Disclosure

CauseDeck is software for advocates, not legal services.

## What the software does

- India-only mode is on by default. Requests to AI providers stay in India.
- AI proposes and the lawyer confirms. Nothing is sent, filed or committed without the lawyer's
  confirmation.
- AI outputs may be wrong. Check every output before relying on it.
- Customer content is not used to train models.
- Citations are never generated. A citation shown in the product comes from the lawyer or from a
  source the software can link to.

## Models used, by provider

| Provider                                               | Models and use                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Amazon Web Services (Bedrock, India inference profile) | Claude models for drafts and summaries.            |
| Sarvam                                                 | Speech-to-text, OCR and Indic-language processing. |
