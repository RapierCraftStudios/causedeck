## Why this page exists

CauseDeck and its public API do not name the portal, aggregator, dataset or route a record came from. The attribution that a licence requires is given here instead, and also next to the text itself where the licence asks for that (see the plain-text dataset below). Records carry the date they were captured (`as_of`) and, where the court publishes its own copy, a link to it (`official_url`).

- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Attribution: "Dattam Labs via overthelex/indian-court-decisions, CC-BY-4.0". We changed the data by redacting names, removing duplicates and extracting metadata and citations from it.

A judgment from the AWS Open Data datasets shows only its court, case number, citation, date and a link to the official copy. A judgment from the plain-text dataset also shows the attribution above wherever its text appears (the judgment reader, search results, copilot source cards and the citations panel), and its page numbers are marked "page pending" until they are confirmed against the court's PDF.

### Court and notice records

Case status, cause lists, orders and notices come from the public records of the courts. They are served only from sources whose terms we have cleared, and each link points to the court's own copy. These records need no further attribution.

## Using the public API

The API terms apply to every record from the [developer API](/developers/api). Where a record carries an `official_url`, the court's copy is the authoritative one.
