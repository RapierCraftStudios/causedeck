## Open judgment data

Part of CauseDeck's judgment search is built from a public dataset of Indian court judgments,
published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC-BY-4.0).

Attribution: Dattam Labs, Indian Supreme Court and High Court Judgments, AWS Open Data, CC-BY-4.0.

The dataset is made available through AWS Open Data. We have changed it: PDF text is extracted and
indexed, and each judgment is matched to its court, case number, citation and date.

## What CauseDeck shows

A judgment in CauseDeck shows its court, case number, citation, date and a link to the official
copy. It does not show which dataset the judgment came from.
