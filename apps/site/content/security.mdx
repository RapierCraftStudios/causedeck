<Section id="data-in-india" title="Data in India">

Your workspace data is stored and processed on infrastructure in India: the app, the database, uploaded
files and the email that the software sends. The [sub-processor list](/legal/sub-processors/) names every
service that handles data, with its region.

This website is separate from the product. It is a static site on Cloudflare Pages and holds no
customer data. The product app is not served through Cloudflare's proxy by default, so matter data does
not pass through edge servers outside India.

</Section>

<Section id="ai-processing" title="AI processing">

India-only AI mode is on by default for every workspace. AI requests go to India-region endpoints
only, through one gateway that applies the same rules everywhere. The AI has no tools: it cannot send,
file or change anything. It returns proposals that you confirm, and the output is checked against a
schema before you see it. Documents are treated as data, never as instructions. Read the full
[AI disclosure](/legal/ai-disclosure/).

</Section>

<Section id="no-training" title="No training on your data">

CauseDeck does not train models on your content. We use AI providers only under terms that exclude
training on it, with no or short retention. Style learning works from your own edits inside your
workspace and does not train a model.

</Section>

<Section id="encryption-and-isolation" title="Encryption and isolation">

Connections use TLS. Documents and memory values are encrypted with a key held for each workspace
alone, so one workspace's key never opens another's data. The database also enforces row-level
isolation between workspaces, and an automated cross-tenant test suite must pass before any change
can ship. Workspace owners sign in with two-factor authentication. Support access is granted by you,
limited in time and recorded; staff do not see your content by default.

</Section>

<Section id="you-stay-in-control" title="You stay in control">

CauseDeck prepares and proposes. It does not send, file or advise. Updates to clients, seniors and
counsel are drafts that you edit and send yourself. Changes the software makes on your behalf appear in
an activity feed and can be undone for seven days.

</Section>

<Section id="audit-and-retention" title="Audit and retention">

Sensitive actions and every AI call are written to an audit trail that cannot be edited afterwards,
and the log is kept out of matter content. Audit and AI-call records are kept for at least 180 days.
Our logs are kept in India.

</Section>

<Section id="export-and-delete" title="Export and delete">

A workspace owner can export everything in the workspace as files you can open without us: tables as
CSV and your documents. An owner can also delete the workspace. Deletion starts
with a 14-day cool-off that any owner can cancel. After it, the workspace key is destroyed and the
data can no longer be read. Statutory records such as issued invoices are kept as the law requires.

</Section>

<Section id="compliance" title="Compliance posture">

- **CERT-In.** We keep a runbook to report cyber incidents to CERT-In within six hours, and we keep
  logs for 180 days within India, as the CERT-In directions require.
- **DPDP Act.** The software is built to support a Data Fiduciary's duties under the Digital Personal
  Data Protection Act: export, deletion, a sub-processor list and a breach process. Your chamber
  remains the Data Fiduciary; see the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa/).
- **Certifications.** We do not claim any security certification today.

</Section>

<Section id="sub-processors" title="Sub-processors">

The services that process data for CauseDeck are listed, with purpose, data categories and region, on
the [sub-processor list](/legal/sub-processors/). A change to it is announced in the change feed.

</Section>

<Section id="sources" title="Every fact shows its source">

When the software shows a date, a direction or a party it has read from an order or cause list, it
shows where in the document it came from, so you can check it against the original. Nothing is
presented as established unless it has a source.

</Section>
