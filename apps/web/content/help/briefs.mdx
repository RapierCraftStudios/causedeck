A brief sheet is a one-page summary of a listing, prepared before the hearing.

## What it holds

- The matter, court, hall and item number.
- The last order and the next date.
- Open obligations and your notes.

## Using it

Open **Briefs** to see sheets for upcoming listings. Print a sheet or open it on your phone in court. Sheets are rebuilt when the matter changes.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
