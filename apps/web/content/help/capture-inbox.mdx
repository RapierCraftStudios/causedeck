Everything that arrives lands in the Inbox as a capture.

## Statuses

- **Processing**: being read.
- **Needs you**: a decision is required.
- **Ready**: waiting to be applied.

## Channels

Email, voice notes, camera photos, WhatsApp and calendar files each have their own opt-in under **Settings, Channels**. You can discard or pin any capture.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
