A clerk can prepare work but cannot change who has access.

## What a clerk can do

- View matters they are given and prepare captures and briefs.
- Propose changes that an advocate accepts.

## What a clerk cannot do

- Invite or remove members, or change roles.
- Export or delete the workspace.
- See sensitive matters unless an advocate includes them.

Owners manage roles from **Settings, Members**.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
