You can take your data out or remove it.

## Export

Owners can request a full export from **Settings, Data**. The export is prepared in the background and a download link is sent when ready.

## Delete

Deleting the workspace starts a grace period during which you can cancel. After it ends, the workspace and its files are erased.

Both actions are recorded in the audit log and need a recent two-factor check.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
