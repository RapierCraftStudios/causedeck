The Court-Day Board is the page you open each morning. It lists today's hearings grouped by court and hall, so you can see where you need to be and in what order.

## Reading the board

- Each row is one listing: matter, court, hall and the item number.
- Live halls show the item now being heard and an estimate of when your item will be reached.
- Bench notices that affect your matters appear above the list.

## If the board is empty

Forward a cause list to your intake address or import one from **Inbox**. Hearings appear here as soon as the list is read.

See also [importing lists and orders](importing-lists-and-orders).
