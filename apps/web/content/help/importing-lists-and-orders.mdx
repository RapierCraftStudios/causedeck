You can bring cause lists and orders into CauseDeck by forwarding them, uploading files or importing a batch.

## Ways in

- **Forward by email** to your private intake address. Only senders on your allow-list are read.
- **Upload files** from Capture. PDFs and photos are read on the server.
- **Bulk import** a folder of past lists to build your matters.

## What happens next

Every file becomes a capture in your Inbox. Nothing changes your matters until you accept the proposals it produces. See [proposals and undo](proposals-and-undo).
