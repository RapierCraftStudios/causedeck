Press **?** anywhere outside a text field to open the shortcut sheet.

## Common keys

- **c** opens capture.
- **g then t** goes to Today.
- **/** focuses the page search.
- **mod+k** opens the command palette.

On touch devices the sheet is replaced by a short gestures guide.
