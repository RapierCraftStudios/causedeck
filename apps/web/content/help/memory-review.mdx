Memory is what the workspace has learned about your matters, such as a judge's usual directions or how a client is addressed.

## Review

- New items wait for your confirmation before they are used.
- Confirm, edit or remove any item.
- Removed items are not suggested again.

## Scope

Memory belongs to your workspace. It is never shared with other workspaces.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
