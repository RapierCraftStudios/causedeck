CauseDeck can alert you about hearings, notices and obligations.

## Choosing what to receive

Open **Settings, Notifications** to pick channels and categories. Quiet hours hold non-urgent alerts until the morning.

The bell opens the latest alerts; press **shift+n** to open it from the keyboard.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
