Directions and undertakings recorded in orders become obligations with a due date.

## The queue

- Overdue items are listed first, then those due soon.
- Each obligation links to the order line it came from.
- Mark an obligation done, or move it with a reason.

## Adding your own

Use **New obligation** on a matter to record something that was agreed in court but is not in an order.

Due dates are computed in Indian Standard Time.
