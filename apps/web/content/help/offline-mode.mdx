CauseDeck keeps working when the connection drops.

## What works offline

- Today's board and your recent matters.
- Help articles, including this one.
- Captures you make are queued and sent when you are back online.

## What needs a connection

Imports, support requests and anything that reads a court website.

A banner tells you when you are offline and when queued items have been sent.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
