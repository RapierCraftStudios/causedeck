Your data stays in India.

## AI processing

- AI features run only on providers in India.
- Sensitive matters are never sent to AI.
- Each AI result shows its source so you can check it.

## Access to your workspace

Staff see no case content by default. A support session shows only metadata, lasts for the time you choose, and is recorded in the audit log.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
