CauseDeck suggests changes as proposals instead of editing your matters silently.

## Proposals

- A proposal shows what would change and the source line it came from.
- Accept it, edit it, or dismiss it.
- Items that need a person go to **Needs you** in the Inbox.

## Undo

Every applied change is recorded in Activity. Open the entry and choose **Undo** to reverse it, together with anything that was applied in the same step.

Undo is available for a limited time after an import; the Activity entry shows the deadline.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
