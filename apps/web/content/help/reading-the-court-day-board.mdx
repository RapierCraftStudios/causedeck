Live hall status shows which item a hall is hearing now.

## How to read it

- **Item now** is the listing currently called.
- **N away** counts the items between that one and yours.
- The estimated window is a guide based on recent pace, not a promise.

Status comes from the court's public display and may lag behind the room.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
