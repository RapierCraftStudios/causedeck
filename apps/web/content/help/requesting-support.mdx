Open the Contact tab in Help to reach us.

## Options

- Chat on WhatsApp during court-day hours.
- Email us when WhatsApp is not available.
- Owners can **Request a support session**.

A support session is time-boxed, shows staff metadata only and ends automatically. You can end it earlier from **Settings, Support**.

This is software documentation, not legal advice.
