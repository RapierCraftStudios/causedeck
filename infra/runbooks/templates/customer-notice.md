# Customer (Data Fiduciary) breach notice - template

CauseDeck is the Processor; the advocate or chamber is the Data Fiduciary. Wording is `Counsel to confirm`
(#117) before first use. A human approves and sends this; no automated outbound messages. Approval is recorded
in the ticket (name, time).

Subject: Security incident affecting your CauseDeck workspace `<workspace name>`

Dear `<name>`,

On `<date, IST>` we detected `<plain description, no speculation>`. We confirmed at `<time, IST>` that it
`<did / did not>` involve your workspace data.

What was involved: `<data categories, or "no client data">` · Period: `<from>` to `<to>`

What we have done: `<containment and recovery steps>` · What you may want to do: `<for example change
passwords, review recent activity>`

We will update you by `<time>`. Contact: `<support channel from the secret manager reference>`.

`<Counsel to confirm: statement on your notification duties as Data Fiduciary and our assistance>`

Approved by: `<name>` at `<time>` · Sent by: `<name>` at `<time>`
