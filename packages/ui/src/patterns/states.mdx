# Pattern states

Every pattern has the same state matrix: default, hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, loading
skeleton, error, offline (read-only) and permission denied. Each pattern's stories show its own
states. The screens below cover a whole surface when there is nothing to show inside it. They are
built on the `EmptyState` and `Skeleton` components.

## Empty

One calm sentence and at most one primary action. Never use an exclamation mark.

- Proposals: `Nothing needs you right now.`
- Activity: `Nothing has happened yet. Forward an order or cause list to get started.`
- Obligations: `No open obligations. Well kept.`

## Loading

`PatternSkeleton` uses the same layout as the pattern it stands in for, so the page does not shift
when data arrives. It is announced once as `Loading`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the shimmer
stops.

## Error

Say what happened in one plain sentence, then offer `Try again` and a support link.

## Offline

Saved data stays readable. The banner reads `You are offline. Showing saved data from <time>.`.
Actions stay visible but disabled, each with a short note that says why.

<Canvas of={StateStories.Offline} />

## Permission denied

Actions are hidden. Inside a pattern, a `View only` note replaces them. A whole surface shows a lock
and `You do not have access to this. Ask the workspace owner.`

## Motion

These are the transitions the patterns use, all taken from the motion tokens:

- A confirmed card collapses on the list spring while its content fades over 200 ms with `--ease-standard`.
- The chip popover fades and rises 4 px over 150 ms (`--dur-fast`).
- The undo toast slides in over 200 ms (`--dur-base`).

Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every one of these is instant.
